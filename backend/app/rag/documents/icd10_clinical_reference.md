# ICD-10 Clinical Classification Reference Document

## Acute & Febrile Conditions
- **R50.9**: Fever, unspecified. (Used when etiology of fever is not yet confirmed by lab testing).
- **R50.81**: Fever presenting with conditions classified elsewhere.
- **A90**: Dengue fever [classical dengue].
- **B34.9**: Viral infection, unspecified.
- **R51**: Headache, unspecified.
- **R53.83**: Other fatigue / generalized malaise.

## Respiratory Diseases
- **J06.9**: Acute upper respiratory infection, unspecified.
- **J02.9**: Acute pharyngitis, unspecified.
- **J00**: Acute nasopharyngitis [common cold].
- **J20.9**: Acute bronchitis, unspecified.
- **R05**: Cough.
- **R06.02**: Shortness of breath / Dyspnea.

## Cardiovascular & Metabolic
- **I10**: Essential (primary) hypertension.
- **I25.10**: Atherosclerotic heart disease of native coronary artery.
- **E11.9**: Type 2 diabetes mellitus without complications.
- **E78.5**: Hyperlipidemia, unspecified.

## Gastrointestinal & General
- **K21.9**: Gastro-esophageal reflux disease without esophagitis.
- **K29.70**: Gastritis, unspecified, without bleeding.
- **R10.9**: Unspecified abdominal pain.
- **R11.0**: Nausea with vomiting.
- **Z88.8**: Allergy status to other drugs, medicaments and biological substances.
