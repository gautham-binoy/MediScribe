# Clinical Guideline: Essential Hypertension & Cardiovascular Risk Assessment

## Diagnostic Criteria
- Normal: < 120/< 80 mmHg.
- Elevated: 120-129/< 80 mmHg.
- Stage 1 Hypertension: 130-139 / 80-89 mmHg confirmed on 2 or more clinical readings.
- Stage 2 Hypertension: >= 140 / >= 90 mmHg.
- Hypertensive Emergency: BP > 180/120 with target organ damage (papilledema, acute kidney injury, encephalopathy, chest pain). Immediate emergency transfer required.

## Essential Workup
1. Fasting blood glucose / HbA1c
2. Lipid panel (Total cholesterol, HDL, LDL, Triglycerides)
3. Serum Creatinine, eGFR, Blood Urea Nitrogen
4. Serum Electrolytes (Sodium, Potassium)
5. 12-lead Electrocardiogram (ECG) for left ventricular hypertrophy (LVH)

## Management Principles
- Lifestyle modification: Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension (DASH) diet (< 2g sodium/day), 150 min/week moderate aerobic exercise, weight reduction.
- First-line pharmacotherapy: Amlodipine 5mg OD (Calcium channel blocker), or Telmisartan 40mg OD (ARB).
- ICD-10 Mappings: I10 (Essential hypertension).
