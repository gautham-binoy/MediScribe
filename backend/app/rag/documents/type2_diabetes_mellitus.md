# Clinical Guideline: Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus Management

## Diagnostic Thresholds
- Fasting Plasma Glucose >= 126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L).
- 2-hour Post-load Plasma Glucose >= 200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) during 75g OGTT.
- HbA1c >= 6.5% (48 mmol/mol).
- Random Plasma Glucose >= 200 mg/dL with classic symptoms of hyperglycemia (polyuria, polydipsia, unexplained weight loss).

## Recommended Routine Testing
- HbA1c quarterly or twice yearly.
- Annual urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (uACR) and serum creatinine for diabetic nephropathy screening.
- Comprehensive dilated eye examination and comprehensive foot examination.

## Pharmacotherapy
- First-line: Metformin 500mg BD with meals, titrated to 1000mg BD as tolerated.
- Caution: Check eGFR before starting metformin; contraindicated if eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m2.
- ICD-10 Mappings: E11.9 (Type 2 diabetes mellitus without complications), E11.65 (Type 2 diabetes mellitus with hyperglycemia).
