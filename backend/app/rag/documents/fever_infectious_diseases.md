# Clinical Guideline: Fever and Acute Infectious Illness in Adults

## Overview
Fever (body temperature > 38.0°C or 100.4°F) is a frequent presentation in primary and outpatient care. Acute febrile illnesses lasting < 7 days in tropical/subtropical climates warrant systematic evaluation for common viral pathogens, dengue, malaria, and upper respiratory tract infections.

## Diagnostic Approach
- **Duration**: Acute (< 7 days), Subacute (1-3 weeks), Chronic (> 3 weeks).
- **Associated Symptoms**: Headache, retro-orbital pain, myalgia, arthralgia, dry/productive cough, sore throat, maculopapular rash.
- **Red Flags / Warning Signs**: Persistent high-grade temperature (> 39.5°C), lethargy, altered mental status, persistent vomiting, abdominal tenderness, mucosal bleeding (epistaxis, gum bleed), petechiae, hemodynamic instability (hypotension, tachycardia).

## Recommended Investigations
1. **Complete Blood Count (CBC)**:
   - Check total leukocyte count (leukopenia common in viral illness/dengue; leukocytosis in bacterial sepsis).
   - Platelet count (thrombocytopenia < 100,000/µL raises suspicion for dengue/malaria).
   - Hematocrit (hemoconcentration indicates plasma leakage in dengue).
2. **Dengue NS1 Antigen & IgM/IgG**:
   - NS1 positive on Days 1-5 of illness.
   - Dengue serology IgM from Day 5 onwards.
3. **Peripheral Blood Smear / Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT) for Malaria**: In endemic regions.
4. **Urine Routine**: If urinary symptoms (dysuria, frequency) are present.

## Symptomatic & Empirical Pharmacotherapy
- **Antipyretic / Analgesic**: Paracetamol 500mg to 650mg orally every 6 to 8 hours as needed (Maximum daily dose: 3000mg to 4000mg in adults with normal hepatic function).
- **CAUTION**: Avoid NSAIDs (e.g., Aspirin, Ibuprofen, Diclofenac) during acute febrile illness of unknown origin or suspected dengue due to increased risk of platelet inhibition, gastritis, and severe hemorrhage.
- **Hydration**: Oral rehydration therapy (minimum 2.5 to 3 liters/day of fluid intake including oral rehydration salts, soups, water).
